05
Let It See Again

Bird-safe film · Let the bird see again

Bird-safe film is a dot pattern applied to the outside of the glass: 5 cm apart, 10 mm dots. It barely affects the view from inside.

Birds can see the dots. They know something is there, and fly around it.

You just watched it hit. Now place the dots, and let this glass appear in its field of view again.

A Shared Sky
Lighting

One bird's death often comes in two stages.

Drawn into the city by light at night, killed by glass in the morning. Migrating birds navigate by stars and magnetism; the lights of a skyline pull them off course.

Halving the lit window area cuts collisions by roughly 11 times in spring and 6 times in autumn.Van Doren et al. 2021, PNAS

Production notes

ENGL7606 Interdisciplinary Multimedia Narratives · Group 5 · prototype for the concept proposal “A Sky to Fly”. The pages above are a runnable first draft. This page lists what each step does and what is still missing.

00 · Removed in this version

The choosing screen is cut. This version opens on 01. The bird is fixed: a Light-vented Bulbul at 32 g, the species the offline balance is built for. Its name and weight first appear at 05.

01 · Cleaning

One continuous wipe, with three turns: a mark appears, the mark is wiped away, the treeline flips to the top of the frame. The chapter title changes from A Sky to Fly to The Invisible Wall. The wipe drives the sound parameters in real time.

02 · Seeing

A hand-drawn paper page. The copy states the double reading first, then the reflection, then the room and the next person who wipes. The closing line is “Wiping them away only lets you see more clearly.”

03 · Falling

The bird turns down like a leaf and lands without a sound. All sound is ducked. The cotton line follows.

04 · The map

A schematic map with three verified sites: Central (a Watercock, September 2024), HKU Centennial Campus (first bird-safe markers, May 2025), and Mai Po (a migration corridor, not a collision site). Other sites are marked pending; no events are invented.

05 · Placing the dots

The audience places the dots and the figures unlock with the hand. The intro block explains the film and ends with “let this glass appear in its field of view again.”

06 · The sticker

A sticker is generated for the fixed bird at a real specification: 5 cm spacing, 10 mm dots, printable.

Noise / Other harm

Noise is about the cost of communication, not a cause of death. Other harm covers light: drawn into the city at night, killed by glass in the morning.

Sound

A slow four-voice pad, one chord per chapter, gliding rather than switching. Birdsong is the only melodic element. The synthesis is a placeholder for real recordings.

Boundary

No real harm is produced. The impact sound stays within safe volume. The sticker specification is real (5 cm / 10 mm). The work does not claim to reconstruct a bird's perception. The cotton is cotton.
